Key Studies & Insights
🌧️

Monsoon Dependency Analysis

All five estates derive 55–60% of annual rainfall from the Southwest Monsoon (June–September). BVE and KNN show the highest monsoon concentration, making them most sensitive to monsoon variability. KMP's broader NE monsoon contribution (28%) offers a more evenly distributed water supply across the year.

📊

Rainfall Intensity Ranking

BVE receives the highest average annual rainfall (2,847 mm) but also has the highest runoff risk. KMP is the driest estate (2,034 mm) with the fewest rainy days (~95/yr), suggesting irrigation investment may be needed during dry spells.

🌱

Agricultural Impact Study

Arabica varieties thrive with the steady rainfall patterns of KNR, GOD, and KMP estates. The heavier monsoon intensity at BVE and KNN supports Robusta intercropping. The January–February dry dormancy period across all estates aligns with the standard post-harvest pruning cycle.

⏳

Seasonal Living Conditions

For residential or hospitality development: BVE and KNN offer the most lush, green environment year-round but may experience waterlogging during peak monsoon. KMP and GOD have drier, more comfortable dry seasons, ideal for year-round habitation and tourism.

🔑 Key Takeaways from the Data:
• BVE is the wettest estate — ideal for water-intensive crops but watch for drainage and soil erosion during peak monsoon (Jul–Aug).
• KMP is the driest — best suited for drought-tolerant varieties; may need supplemental irrigation in Jan–Mar.
• All estates share a common monsoon rhythm: Southwest monsoon (Jun–Sep) drives 55–60% of annual rainfall. This uniformity means estate selection has more to do with elevation and soil than rainfall alone.
• NE monsoon contribution is highest at KMP (28%) and GOD (25%) — these estates get a useful "second wind" of rainfall in Oct–Nov, beneficial for late-season crop development.
