06 — Key Insights & Observations
📈
Gowri leads overall — highest cumulative rainfall (312 mm) driven by the most consistent monthly distribution across all seasons. Ideal for year-round operations.
⚡
Hidden Falls has the most intense events — highest single-day rainfall (186 mm) recorded, but fewer rainy days. Flash-flood risk during peak monsoon.
🌵
Vyapurikuttai is the driest — lowest total (165 mm) and longest dry spells (10+ days). Better suited for drought-resistant coffee varietals.
📊
2026 trending above 2025 — 4 of 6 estates show positive year-over-year growth. Hidden Falls leading with +17.1% increase. Moganad is the only estate showing a decline.
🎯
Monsoon concentration — 78% of annual rainfall falls between June and November. Estates with better drainage (Gowri, Orchardale) show more stable productivity.
🗓
Peak window: July–October — This 4-month window delivers 60–70% of annual rainfall across all estates. Critical period for harvest planning and processing capacity.
💧
Average event intensity increases west-to-east — Vyapurikuttai (13.8 mm/event) → Gowri (8.2 mm/event). Western estates get heavier but less frequent rain.
