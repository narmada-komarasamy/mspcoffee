💡 Section 8 — Proposed New Infographics & Studies

New chart types that can be added to the existing infographic page. Each builds on the live Supabase data already connected.

🗺️
Interactive Estate Map
Clickable SVG/Mapbox map of all 6 estates. Color-coded by rainfall. Tooltips show live stats per estate. Click to filter the entire infographic.
NEW
⛰️
Rainfall vs Elevation Study
Scatter plot + trend line showing orographic lift. Higher estates receive more rain. Requires elevation data input. R² correlation included.
RESEARCH
🕸️
Seasonal Radar Charts
Spider charts with 12 monthly axes. Overlay estates to compare seasonal profiles at a glance. Instantly reveals which estates have complementary rainfall patterns.
NEW
📡
Rainfall Anomaly Detector
2σ statistical analysis flagging unusual months. Color-coded severity: yellow (mild), orange (moderate), red (extreme). Proactive planning alerts.
RESEARCH
📅
Monsoon Onset & Retreat Calendar
First/last rain date per year. Trend arrows show shift direction. Useful for adjusting planting schedules over time.
NEW
☕
Crop Suitability Scorecard
SCA-guideline-based scores: ideal 1,500–2,500mm, dry spells <30 days. Links weather data directly to crop decisions. Likely outcome: Gowri & Moganad optimal, Vyapurikuttai marginal.
RESEARCH
📈
5-Year Trend Lines with Regression
Linear regression for each estate over 5 years. Shows which estates are getting wetter/drier. Includes confidence intervals and slope annotations.
ENHANCED
🔍
Resolution Toggle: Daily vs Monthly
Toggle between daily granularity (showing individual downpour events) and monthly totals. Demonstrates how aggregation masks extreme weather.
NEW
📊
Rainfall Distribution Histograms
Frequency of 0–10mm, 10–20mm, 20–50mm, 50–100mm, 100mm+ rain days. Gaussian overlay shows distribution normality per estate.
RESEARCH
🌀
ENSO Correlation Study
Overlays El Niño / La Niña phases on rainfall. Expected: El Niño 15–25% drier, La Niña 10–20% wetter. Useful for long-range planning.
RESEARCH
📆
Daily Rainfall Calendar Heatmap
GitHub-style calendar. Each cell = one day, colored by rain intensity. Reveals daily, weekly, seasonal rain rhythm. Hover for exact mm.
NEW
⚖️
Estate Water Balance Calculator
Rainfall minus estimated evapotranspiration (from Open-Meteo temperature). Shows surplus/deficit months per estate. Helps with irrigation planning.
NEW
