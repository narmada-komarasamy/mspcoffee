🚀 Section 2: Proposed New Infographics NEW IDEAS

16 new visualizations that would significantly enhance the rainfall analytics dashboard. Each includes a description, data requirements, and visual mockup concept.

1. Interactive Estate Map
A visual map of all 6 estates with color-coded rainfall intensity overlays. Click any estate to drill into its detailed charts. Uses SVG-based map with estate boundaries.
Data needed: Estate coordinates (lat/lng), boundaries
Complexity: High · Impact: Very High
2. Rainfall vs Elevation Correlation
Scatter plot comparing elevation of each estate zone vs rainfall received. Reveals orographic rainfall patterns — do higher estates get more rain?
Data needed: Elevation data per estate zone
Complexity: Medium · Impact: High
3. Estate Radar / Spider Charts
Multi-axis radar chart comparing estates across 6 dimensions: Total Rainfall, Rainy Days, Peak Intensity, Seasonal Consistency, Dry Streak Risk, and YoY Growth.
Data needed: Derived from existing rainfall data
Complexity: Medium · Impact: High
4. Rainfall Anomaly Detector
Flags unusual rainfall events — days with 3x+ normal rainfall or zero rain in peak monsoon. Uses statistical thresholds (mean ± 2σ) to highlight anomalies on a timeline.
Data needed: Daily rainfall records + statistical baseline
Complexity: Medium · Impact: Very High
5. Monsoon Onset & Withdrawal Calendar
Timeline visualization showing when SW and NE monsoons start and end at each estate. Color-coded bars for onset, peak, and withdrawal periods.
Data needed: Daily rainfall with monsoon onset algorithm
Complexity: Medium · Impact: High
6. Crop Suitability Scorecard
Derives a coffee-growing suitability index per estate based on rainfall distribution, dry spell length, and monsoon reliability. Scores from 1-10 with recommendations.
Data needed: Rainfall + temperature (from weather API)
Complexity: High · Impact: Very High
7. Extended 5-Year Trend Analysis ENHANCED
Enhanced version of the existing annual bar chart with trend lines, moving averages, and linear regression projections for the next 2 years.
Data needed: 5+ years of rainfall history
Complexity: Low · Impact: Medium
8. Daily vs Monthly Distribution
Dual histogram showing the distribution of daily rainfall values AND monthly totals. Reveals whether rain comes in many light showers or few heavy downpours.
Data needed: Daily rainfall records
Complexity: Medium · Impact: Medium
9. Rainfall Distribution Histogram
Frequency distribution of daily rainfall amounts across all estates. Shows how often different rainfall intensities occur (drizzle vs moderate vs heavy vs extreme).
Data needed: Daily rainfall records
Complexity: Low · Impact: Medium
10. ENSO Climate Correlation RESEARCH
Overlays El Niño / La Niña / Neutral years on the rainfall timeline. Shows how ENSO phases affect MSP Coffee estates differently.
Data needed: Historical ENSO index data (NOAA)
Complexity: High · Impact: High
11. Interactive Comparison Dashboard
Side-by-side estate comparison with select-and-compare. Pick 2-3 estates and view synchronized charts with delta values. Like a "vs" mode for estates.
Data needed: Existing rainfall data
Complexity: Medium · Impact: Very High
12. Water Balance Calculator
Calculates water surplus/deficit per month per estate. Estimates soil moisture availability and irrigation needs based on rainfall vs evapotranspiration.
Data needed: Rainfall + temperature + humidity (weather API)
Complexity: High · Impact: Very High
13. Calendar Heatmap
GitHub-style calendar grid showing daily rainfall intensity for the full year. Each cell is a day, colored by rainfall amount. Spot dry/wet patterns at a glance.
Data needed: Daily rainfall records
Complexity: Low · Impact: Medium
14. Statistical Summary Panel ENHANCED
Expanded version of existing KPIs with percentiles (P10, P25, P50, P75, P90), standard deviation, skewness, and kurtosis for each estate's rainfall distribution.
Data needed: Existing rainfall data
Complexity: Low · Impact: Medium
15. One-Click Report Export
Generate a formatted PDF or Excel report with all charts and summary tables. Scheduled monthly email reports to stakeholders with estate rainfall summaries.
Data needed: Existing data + PDF generation library
Complexity: Medium · Impact: High
16. Rainfall Alert System
Configurable thresholds per estate — alert when weekly rainfall drops below X% of average, or exceeds flood-risk levels. Push notifications for irrigation decisions.
Data needed: Real-time feed (Open-Meteo already integrated)
Complexity: High · Impact: Very High

🔬 Section 3: Research Studies & Deep-Dive Analyses RESEARCH

Advanced analytical studies that leverage the rainfall data to provide actionable agricultural intelligence for MSP Coffee.

📈 Study 1: Climate Trend Analysis (10-Year)
Objective: Identify long-term rainfall patterns and climate shifts affecting MSP estates.

Methodology: Mann-Kendall trend test, Sen's slope estimator, and moving average analysis on 10+ years of data. Detect whether total rainfall is increasing, decreasing, or shifting seasonally.

Output: Trend confidence intervals, projected rainfall for next 5 years, season shift detection (e.g., monsoon arriving later).

Business Value: Long-term planning for crop varieties, irrigation infrastructure, and estate expansion.
🏔️ Study 2: Orographic Rainfall & Elevation Impact
Objective: Quantify how elevation affects rainfall across MSP estates.

Methodology: Collect elevation data for each estate zone. Plot rainfall vs elevation regression. Calculate orographic enhancement factor (rainfall increase per 100m elevation gain).

Output: Elevation-rainfall relationship curves, windward vs leeward analysis, optimal planting zones.

Business Value: Guide new planting decisions, understand which estate zones are most resilient to dry years.
🌊 Study 3: Monsoon Failure Risk Assessment
Objective: Assess the probability and impact of weak or failed monsoons on each estate.

Methodology: Historical monsoon onset/withdrawal analysis, SPI (Standardized Precipitation Index) calculation, Markov chain models for dry/wet spell transitions.

Output: Probability of monsoon failure per decade, critical dry spell periods, water stress timelines.

Business Value: Drought preparedness planning, insurance decisions, irrigation scheduling.
🌡️ Study 4: Rainfall-Temperature-Yield Correlation
Objective: Connect weather patterns to coffee yield and cup quality outcomes.

Methodology: Cross-correlate rainfall and temperature data with processing yields and cup score records. Identify optimal weather windows for cherry maturation.

Output: Weather-yield correlation coefficients, optimal growing condition profiles, yield prediction models.

Business Value: Data-driven quality improvement, harvest timing optimization, variety selection.
🌍 Study 5: ENSO Impact on Indian Monsoon
Objective: Quantify how El Niño and La Niña events affect rainfall at MSP estates.

Methodology: Overlay historical ENSO index (ONI) data from NOAA with MSP rainfall records. Classify years as El Niño, La Niña, or Neutral. Compare rainfall statistics across phases.

Output: ENSO-rainfall correlation maps, probabilistic forecasts, early warning system integration.

Business Value: Seasonal forecasting, resource allocation, risk management.
💧 Study 6: Water Footprint & Sustainability Analysis
Objective: Calculate the water footprint of coffee production at each estate.

Methodology: Combine rainfall data with evapotranspiration estimates (using temperature data). Calculate effective rainfall, irrigation water requirements, and water use efficiency per kg of green coffee.

Output: Water footprint per estate, irrigation efficiency scores, sustainability benchmarks.

Business Value: Sustainability reporting, water resource management, organic certification support.
